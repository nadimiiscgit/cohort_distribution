Questions you might have

What does it cost?

Nothing. There is no paid tier on this bot.

What do you store about me?

Your Telegram chat ID, the link you arrived from, and which questions you've answered — that's what makes it possible to not send you the same question twice. Nothing is sold or shared. Send /stop and the messages stop.

How many questions can I get?

Up to five a day, on demand. Send /question whenever you want the next one.

Can I pick a subject?

Yes — /question Pharmacology pulls from that subject alone.
